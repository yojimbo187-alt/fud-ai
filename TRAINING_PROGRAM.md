# Ruoka + Treeni training-program methodology

The priority program is the five-day Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower block recovered from Setwise Advanced. Each exercise slot offers five ranked substitutions. The first option is the default, but the user's selection is saved independently for each program and day.

## What “science-ranked” means

The order is an evidence-informed starting point, not a claim that one implement is universally best. The catalog favors:

- large-muscle, multi-joint work early in the session;
- stable exercises that make progressive overload easy to measure;
- a comfortable full range of motion, with lengthened loading where practical;
- direct loading of the target muscle; and
- individual comfort, equipment access, and performance when choosing a substitution.

The implementation is informed by research on [exercise order](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30248269/), [range of motion](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34170576/), [longer-muscle-length training](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36662126/), and [machine versus free-weight training](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37582807/). These findings support the ranking criteria, but they do not establish a permanent universal ranking of individual exercises.

## Progression block

The program runs as four repeating four-week waves across 16 weeks. Training weeks use three working sets with 3, 2, then 1 reps in reserve. Every fourth week is a deload using two working sets at 4 reps in reserve. Rep ranges and rest guidance are stored per exercise slot and shown identically on iOS and Android.
