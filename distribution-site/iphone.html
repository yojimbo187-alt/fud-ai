IPHONE · FREE PERSONAL INSTALL

Install with AltStore

This uses Apple’s free personal signing. You need a Windows 10 or newer PC and your own Apple Account.

Before you start

The app expires after seven days unless AltStore refreshes it while your iPhone and PC are connected to the same network. The personal build removes the Watch, widget, Share and iCloud extensions that free signing cannot authorize. Core tracking remains native; HealthKit availability depends on Apple’s personal provisioning.

  1. 1

    Install AltServer on Windows

    Download AltServer from the official AltStore website and follow its Windows installation guide.

    Open official AltStore download
  2. 2

    Connect your iPhone

    Connect by USB, unlock the phone, choose Trust, and enable Wi-Fi sync in Apple Devices or iTunes. Install AltStore to the phone from the AltServer tray icon.

  3. 3

    Download the personal IPA

    Save the Ruoka + Treeni personal build in Safari or your Files app.

    Download iPhone IPA
  4. 4

    Open it in AltStore

    Open AltStore on the iPhone, choose My Apps → +, select the downloaded IPA and sign in with your Apple Account when AltStore requests it.

  5. 5

    Refresh every seven days

    Keep AltServer running periodically while the phone is on the same Wi-Fi. In AltStore, use Refresh All before the seven-day timer expires.

Ohjeet suomeksi

Asenna ensin AltServer Windows-tietokoneelle, yhdistä iPhone USB-kaapelilla ja asenna AltStore puhelimeen. Lataa sitten IPA tältä sivulta, avaa AltStore, paina My Apps → + ja valitse IPA. Uusi allekirjoitus AltStoressa seitsemän päivän välein.
